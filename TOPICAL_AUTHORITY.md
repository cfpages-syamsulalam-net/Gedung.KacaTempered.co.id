# Topical Authority — gedung.kacatempered.co.id

## Role and boundary

This domain should become a practical reference for owners, facility teams, consultants, and contractors managing glass in occupied buildings: identifying the installed system, inspecting condition, cleaning safely, maintaining interfaces, choosing film retrofits, diagnosing defects, and deciding when engineered repair or replacement is required. It supports the existing cleaning and window-film services without turning neutral guidance into sales pages. Structural adequacy, glass thickness, wind/load design, impact or fall protection, fire performance, work-at-height methods, and compliance claims require project-specific drawings, applicable Indonesian requirements, manufacturer documentation, test reports, and competent professional review; articles must never present generic dimensions or DIY work as approval.

- The local homepage title and H1 identify the offer as certified building-glass care and maintenance. Visible headings emphasize services, coverage, dirty/dull/cracked glazed walls, and selecting building window film.
- Meaningful existing commercial routes are `/cleaning-kaca` and `/stiker-kaca-film`, with child pages for 3M, V-Kool, and Solar Gard. Proposed guides must educate and qualify decisions, then link to a commercial route only when relevant.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub. GKG-03 survey pages lead to the relevant GKG-04 diagnosis, GKG-07 maintenance action, and GKG-13 escalation decision. GKG-09 selection pages link to GKG-10 compatibility/defect guidance and, only for qualified purchase intent, the existing `/stiker-kaca-film` route. GKG-05 cleaning guidance may link to `/cleaning-kaca` after method and risk selection. Application pages in GKG-15 link back to material behavior (GKG-02), access safety (GKG-06), interfaces (GKG-08), and evidence verification (GKG-16). No planned article is duplicated by a city name.
